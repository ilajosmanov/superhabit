# bootstrap

The app's start, above every feature and below the routes.

| File                            | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `startup.ts`                    | the machine the root layout runs: migrate, sign in (once; again after the server ends the session), ask about sharing traces (while this phone has no answer for the current policy), seed, bring back what your account holds (one try of 30 s at most, until a pull has), offer back a workout left open, keep today, write freezes of days that are over and delete videos past 30 days, check the session; after Delete account, clear the phone |
| `keep-synced.ts`                | `keepSynced`, the actor that sends and pulls while you're signed in (at the start, when the app or the network comes back), sends after a change is queued, then shares traces; slower retries                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `day.ts`                        | `dayClock`, the actor that tells the day at midnight, when the app returns, and within a minute of a time zone change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `left-open.ts`                  | `useLeftOpen()`: the workout startup found open; a page linked at launch waits for its Save or Discard                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `leave.ts`                      | `useLeave()`: Profile's Sign out and Delete account, and Sign in again after the session ended, sent to the startup machine                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `today.ts`                      | `useToday()`: the day for screens (never `today()` while rendering)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `crash-reports.ts`              | starts Sentry before anything else runs: crashes, errors and a tenth of navigations, nothing about the person                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `appearance.ts`                 | wears the theme you chose (light, dark or the phone's) at load, before the first frame; `chooseTheme` for Profile                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `components/feedback-sheet.tsx` | the crash screen's Send feedback: a sheet with one field, sent to Sentry with the crash it follows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

`startup.ts` only orders the steps; each lives with its owner: opening and migrating the database in
`storage/`, signing in and the session in `account/`, seeding our challenges in `challenges/`, sending and pulling in `account/sync.ts`, finding and saving or discarding a workout left
open through `workout/store.ts` and the machine in `workout/recovery.ts`. The store also owns startup
file cleanup, which waits until a pull has brought back the sets a video belongs to. After checking the database, `reading` mounts the shared `LibraryProvider` once. It gates
its children until every live read has first answered; its wrapper's `onLayout` then reports `dataReady` once the
screens under it are laid out. Startup can then enter `ready` (or offer recovery) and hide the splash. No event is sent
during render, and the provider stays mounted through recovery and normal navigation. A failed step
shows the error and Retry; the machine hides the native splash only once, including after recovery or retry.
